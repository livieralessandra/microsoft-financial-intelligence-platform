# Product Principles

These principles guide every product decision throughout the Microsoft Financial Intelligence Platform.

---

## 1. Understanding over Information

Users should leave with a clear understanding of Microsoft's business—not just more financial data.

If a feature presents information without improving understanding, it should be reconsidered.

---

## 2. Reduce Cognitive Load

Financial information should be easier to consume than the original source.

The platform should simplify complexity without sacrificing accuracy.

---

## 3. Explain the "Why"

Showing what changed is not enough.

Whenever possible, explain why it changed and why it matters.

---

## 4. Business Before Technology

Users care about business insights.

Python, SQL, AI, and Power BI are implementation details—not product features.

---

## 5. Executive First

The most important information should be visible within seconds.

Users should immediately understand Microsoft's overall business performance before exploring details.

---

## 6. Every Visualization Must Answer a Question

Charts should exist only if they help users answer a meaningful business question.

Avoid charts created solely because the data is available.

---

## 7. Build Trust

Every insight should be traceable back to Microsoft's official financial disclosures.

Users should always know where information comes from.

---

## 8. AI Assists, It Doesn't Replace Thinking

AI should explain, summarize, and surface insights.

Final interpretation and decision-making remain with the user.

---

## 9. Progressive Disclosure

Start simple.

Allow users to explore deeper levels of information only when they want more detail.

---

## 10. Design for Decisions

The goal is not to display data.

The goal is to help users make better business decisions faster.
